# Privacy Policy for Tap'n'Eat

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-01

## Overview

Tap'n'Eat ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you play our game.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

- **IP Address**: We detect your country based on your IP address to show regional leaderboard rankings. This is done through a third-party service (ipapi.co) and the result is cached on your device for 24 hours. Your IP address is not stored by us.
- **Game Scores**: When you submit a score, the score, the nickname you chose, and your country are stored in our Firebase database for the global leaderboard.
- **Nickname**: The leaderboard shows a nickname, not your real name. It defaults to "Anonymous", and you can leave it blank to submit anonymously.
- **Advertising**: We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device information and identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy policy. See "Advertising and children" below.

### Local Storage

- **High Scores**: Your personal high score and game statistics (games played, total score) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.
- **Settings**: Your sound and difficulty preferences are stored locally.
- **Country**: The country detected from your IP address is cached locally for 24 hours so the lookup does not repeat on every submission.

### Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not ask for or store your real name, email address, postal address, or phone number.
- We do not use your Google account name or Google account photo on the leaderboard.
- We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or files.
- We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services.

## How We Use Information

- **Leaderboard**: Your score, nickname, and country are displayed on our public leaderboard, filtered by country and difficulty level.
- **Advertising**: Ads are shown through Google AdMob, described below.

Device performance settings (for example, reducing effects on lower-powered devices) are chosen on your device from basic device information. That information is not transmitted to us.

## Third-Party Services

Our game uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Google AdMob | Advertising | [AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| Firebase | Leaderboard Database | [Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| ipapi.co | Country Detection | [ipapi Privacy](https://ipapi.co/privacy/) |
| Google Play Games Services | Sign-in and player identity | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |

Google Play Games Services is used only to identify your player account so that a single player's entries can be kept consistent. Your Google account name is **not** shown on the leaderboard. Only the nickname described above is displayed.

## Advertising and Children

Tap'n'Eat is declared to Google Play as an app designed for children. Ad requests from the app are tagged as child-directed, which means:

- Ads are not personalised.
- The Android advertising ID is not used, and the app is built without the advertising-ID permission.
- Google blocks ad sources that are not self-certified for child-directed apps under Google Play's Families policy.

## Children's Privacy

Tap'n'Eat is designed for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable data protection laws.

- The only information published about a player is a nickname, a score, and a country. There is no real name, email address, photo, or account name.
- The nickname defaults to "Anonymous". Leaving it blank also submits as "Anonymous".
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is needed to operate the leaderboard.
- We do not require an account to play. Signing in with Google Play Games Services is optional and is used only for player identity, not to publish account details.
- Parents or guardians can contact us at the address below to review or delete any leaderboard entry.

If you believe a child has submitted information that should be removed, contact us and we will delete it.

## Data Retention

- **Leaderboard Scores**: Stored until you request deletion.
- **Local Data**: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
- **IP Address**: Not stored by us. Country lookups are cached on your device for 24 hours and then discarded.

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

The leaderboard is public by design, so anything you enter as a nickname is visible to other players. Please do not enter a real name or other personal information as a nickname.
