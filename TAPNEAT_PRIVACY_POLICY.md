# Privacy Policy for Tap'n'Eat

**Effective Date:** 2026-04-03

## Overview

Tap'n'Eat ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you play our game.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- **IP Address**: We detect your country based on your IP address to show regional leaderboard rankings. This is done through a third-party service (ipapi.co) and is cached for 24 hours.
- **Game Scores**: Your game scores and player name are stored in our Firebase database for the global leaderboard.
- **Device Information**: Basic device information such as device type and operating system for game optimization.

### Local Storage
- **High Scores**: Your personal high score and game statistics are stored locally on your device.
- **Settings**: Your sound and difficulty preferences are stored locally.

## How We Use Information

- **Leaderboard**: Your scores and player name are displayed on our public leaderboard, filtered by country and difficulty level.
- **Game Statistics**: We track anonymous game statistics (games played, total scores) to improve gameplay.
- **Advertising**: We use AdMob for advertisements, which may collect device and usage data in accordance with their privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

Tap'n'Eat is designed for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable data protection laws.

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.
- We use kid-safe advertising through Kidoz and AdMob.
- Parents can contact us to review or delete any collected information.

## Data Retention

- **Leaderboard Scores**: Stored indefinitely until you request deletion.
- **Local Data**: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request access to your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data from our leaderboard
- Opt out of leaderboard participation by using "Anonymous" as your name
